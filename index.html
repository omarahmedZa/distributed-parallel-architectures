Distributed and Parallel Architectures

A rigorous, visualization-rich course for students with basic systems/programming background. Learn architectural design decisions, advanced mechanisms, trade-offs, scalability, and open research problems—from parallel foundations to exascale and emerging paradigms.

Fundamentals · Prerequisites (taught first)

1 module
  1. F0Fundamentals of Parallel and Distributed ComputingParallelism basics & Flynn’s taxonomy; shared vs distributed memory; cache/memory hierarchy (→ L2); MPI & OpenMP basics (→ L5); speedup & efficiency (→ L9).Prereq

Module 1 · Advanced Parallel Computer Architectures

3 lectures
  1. 01Advanced Parallel Architecture DesignEvolution of parallel architectures; multicore and many-core systems; architectural trade-offs; performance and scalability.
  2. 02Advanced Memory Architectures and Memory HierarchyCache coherence; MESI; NUMA; memory consistency models; shared-memory challenges.
  3. 03Interconnection Networks and Communication ArchitecturesMesh, Torus, Fat-Tree, Hypercube; switching; routing; latency and bandwidth trade-offs.

Module 2 · Distributed Systems Architecture

3 lectures
  1. 04Distributed Computing Architectures and ModelsDistributed architectures; client-server; peer-to-peer; cluster; grid; distributed memory models.
  2. 05Advanced Message Passing and Communication MechanismsAdvanced MPI; collective and non-blocking communication; optimization; communication overhead.
  3. 06Distributed Synchronization and ConsistencyClock synchronization; mutual exclusion; distributed coordination; consistency models.

Module 3 · Advanced Parallel Processing and Optimization

3 lectures
  1. 07Advanced Parallel Algorithms and Decomposition TechniquesDomain/task decomposition; parallel sorting; graph processing; parallel matrix operations.
  2. 08Load Balancing and Dynamic Resource SchedulingStatic/dynamic load balancing; task scheduling; work stealing; resource allocation strategies.
  3. 09Performance Modeling, Scalability and OptimizationStrong/weak scaling; Amdahl’s & Gustafson’s laws; communication-to-computation ratio; profiling; scalability limits.

Module 4 · Heterogeneous and Emerging Architectures

2 lectures
  1. 10Heterogeneous Computing Architectures: CPU, GPU and AcceleratorsCPU–GPU architectures; heterogeneous memory; GPU clusters; accelerator-based and hybrid parallel designs.
  2. 11Cloud, Edge and Serverless Distributed ComputingCloud HPC; resource management; virtualization/containerization; edge computing; dynamic scaling.

Module 5 · Advanced Research Topics

2 lectures
  1. 12Fault Tolerance, Reliability and Energy-Efficient ComputingFault tolerance; checkpointing/recovery; resilient architectures; power-aware and energy-efficient computing.
  2. 13Future of Distributed and Parallel ArchitecturesExascale; AI-driven HPC; quantum and neuromorphic architectures; emerging research challenges.

In each lecture, read objectives and core mechanisms, study diagrams/tables and worked examples, reflect on trade-offs/limitations and research problems, answer check-for-understanding prompts, and complete the Research Activities (papers, comparison, case study) critically.
